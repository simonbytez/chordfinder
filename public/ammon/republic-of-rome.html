Republic of Rome Tutorial

Fifteen short, hands-on lessons on a drawn copy of the game board. A tutor explains each rule as you use it, and you look things up on the board's own tables. When you finish, you'll be ready for the Early Republic scenario.

Every rule here was checked against the 2nd edition rulebook, and each step cites its section number. The board's tables were copied from the 2nd edition board. Senator numbers in the examples are made up: read the real ones off your cards.

Made as a study aid. Not affiliated with the game's publishers.

    Board tables

    The tables printed on the game board, cut down to what the Early Republic basic game uses. Keep this open while you play.

    Left out on purpose: the Middle and Late Republic columns, and everything for the advanced rules (Pontifex Maximus, Priests, Advocates, trial appeals, Provincial Wars, Rebel Governors and Passing Laws).
